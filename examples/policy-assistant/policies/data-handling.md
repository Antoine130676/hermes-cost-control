# Data Handling and AI Tools Policy (Example Co)

This is a fictional policy written for a demo. Do not treat any rule as real.

## Classifying data
Data is public, internal, confidential or restricted. Restricted data includes personal data, payroll, health information and passwords. When unsure, treat the data as one level higher.

## Using AI tools
Only use AI tools on the approved list. Never paste restricted data, such as customer personal data or payroll, into any AI tool. Confidential data may only go into tools approved for confidential data. Ask the Privacy team before connecting a new tool to company data.

## Personal data requests
If a customer or employee asks to see or delete their personal data, forward the request to the Privacy team within two working days. The company must answer within 30 days.

## Reporting a security incident
Report a suspected security incident, such as clicking a phishing link, losing a laptop or sending data to the wrong person, to the Security team within 24 hours. Do not try to fix it alone and do not delete anything.

## Retention
Payroll records are kept for seven years. Recruitment data is deleted six months after the hiring decision. Customer support tickets are kept for three years.

## Sharing files outside the company
Share files with outside parties only through encrypted links that expire after 14 days. Never send restricted data by ordinary email.
