# Expenses Policy (Example Co)

This is a fictional policy written for a demo. Do not treat any figure as real.

## Who this applies to
All employees and contractors who spend company money or claim money back.

## Meals and client entertainment
Employees travelling on business can claim meals up to 40 euros per day. A client dinner can cost up to 80 euros per person and needs the manager's approval before the dinner, not after. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Travel
Book economy class for flights under six hours. Business class for flights over six hours needs director approval before booking. Choose rail when the journey is under four hours. Hotels are reimbursed up to 150 euros per night in cities and 110 euros per night elsewhere.

## Receipts and deadlines
Submit an expense claim within 30 days of the spend. Any item over 25 euros needs an itemised receipt. If a receipt is lost, complete the lost receipt form and ask your manager to sign it.

## Mileage
Use of a private car for approved business trips is paid at 0.30 euros per kilometre. Keep a log with the date, route and purpose of each trip.

## Approval limits
A manager can approve an expense claim up to 250 euros. The department head approves claims between 250 and 2,000 euros. The finance director approves anything above 2,000 euros.

## Corporate cards
Company cards are for business spend only. Personal purchases are not allowed, even if repaid later. Report a lost or stolen card within 24 hours.
