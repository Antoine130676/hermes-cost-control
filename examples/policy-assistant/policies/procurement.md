# Procurement Policy (Example Co)

This is a fictional policy written for a demo. Do not treat any figure as real.

## When to involve procurement
Purchases above 1,000 euros need a written quote. Purchases above 10,000 euros need three quotes and a review by the procurement team before any commitment is made.

## New software tools
Before buying or trialling a new software tool, get a security review, a privacy review and sign-off from the budget owner. A free trial that uses company data counts as a new tool.

## Contracts and signatures
Only authorised signatories can sign contracts. The finance director must sign any contract above 25,000 euros. Employees must not sign on behalf of the company without written authority.

## Supplier onboarding
Verify a supplier's bank details by phoning a known number, never a number from the invoice. Changes to bank details must be confirmed the same way.

## Gifts from suppliers
Do not accept gifts worth more than 50 euros. Declare any gift above 20 euros to your manager. Cash gifts are never allowed.
