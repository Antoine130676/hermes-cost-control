# Leave Policy (Example Co)

This is a fictional policy written for a demo. Do not treat any figure as real.

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee gets 25 working days of annual leave (holiday) per year. Up to five unused days can be carried into the next year and must be used by 31 March.

## Sick leave
Tell your manager before the start of your working day when you are sick. A doctor's certificate is needed after three consecutive days of sickness. The first five sick days in a year are paid in full.

## Parental leave
The primary caregiver gets 16 weeks of paid parental leave. The second parent gets four weeks of paid leave. Leave can start up to four weeks before the expected birth or adoption.

## Requesting leave
Request leave in the HR portal. For absences longer than five working days, request at least two weeks ahead. Your manager answers within three working days.

## Unpaid leave
Up to four weeks of unpaid leave in a year can be approved by a director for personal reasons.

## Bereavement
Five days of paid leave for the death of an immediate family member and two days for another close relative or friend.

## Public holidays
Public holidays are not counted as annual leave. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, there is no replacement day.
