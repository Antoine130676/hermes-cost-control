# Remote Work Policy (Example Co)

This is a fictional policy written for a demo. Do not treat any figure as real.

## Eligibility and days
Employees can work remotely up to three days a week. Each team agrees on fixed office days so people overlap. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time, when everyone should be reachable.

## Working from abroad
You can work from another EU country for up to 30 days a year with your manager's approval. Working outside the EU needs approval from HR and a tax review, and requires at least six weeks' notice.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop. Every employee gets a home office budget of 400 euros once every three years for a desk, chair or monitor. Keep the receipts.

## Security at home
Use the company VPN on any public Wi-Fi. Lock your screen when you step away. Family members and visitors must not use a work device.

## Coworking spaces
A coworking space can be reimbursed up to 150 euros a month if the manager agrees and the space has a private meeting option.
